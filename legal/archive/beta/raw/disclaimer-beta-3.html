Hop is a travel reference, not advice. It shows general published guidance on visas, entry rules, travel advisories and currency to help you plan. It is not legal, immigration, financial or travel advice, and using Hop creates no advisory relationship.
Always verify before you travel. Rules change without notice, and despite careful verification any entry in Hop can be incomplete or out of date. Before you book, and again before you fly, confirm the current requirements with the destination’s embassy, consulate or official immigration website — every destination card links your own government’s travel advice, and Premium adds the destination’s own visa portal — and with your airline, who apply the rules at check-in. Each destination shows when its entry rules were last verified.
Hop is independent. Hop is not affiliated with, endorsed by or approved by any government or government agency. Advisory levels are the published assessments of the seven governments listed under Travel advisory sources; they belong to those governments, and Hop reproduces the level without adding to it.
